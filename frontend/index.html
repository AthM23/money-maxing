04 · the receipts

NorthwindBench

Every model takes the same exam: 120 financial documents it has never seen. perfect answers means the whole answer was right to the cent. Muse is told the exact answer format on every question. Our model is not, because training taught it the format.

Outside check: on a public invoice benchmark we did not write, graded by their own script, our model got 15 of 18 customers exact to the penny, with every trap document correctly thrown out. And on a brand-new exam generated after training, 614 documents that did not exist before: 0.964 accuracy, matching the original test, so nothing is memorized.
