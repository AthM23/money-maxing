# Customer anchors: say these lines, close the judges' biggest ask

Judge feedback v2, section 3: anchor the demo in Maximor's actual customers and their pain points.
Maximor's site shows its finance teams: Instructure, Sierra, Groupon, Quantum, Synthesia, Kiteworks,
DURA, eightfold.ai, HiBid. We cannot know their private books, so the honest framing is "a business
shaped like X", tied to what those businesses verifiably are. One line per demo beat.

## Beat: cash application (the Vossberg wire)
Say: "Maximor's customer wall has companies like **Groupon** and **HiBid** on it: consumer marketplaces
that eat thousands of payments with bank fees, currency conversion and short-pays where the reason lives
in somebody's inbox. That is exactly this beat: one wire, three causes, code explains two of them and a
person is asked once about the third."

## Beat: month-end accruals (Castlegate flat rent, Harborline moving usage)
Say: "A business shaped like **Instructure** or **Synthesia** runs on hosting and infrastructure that
moves with usage. The July bill has not arrived at close. Averaging it is how books go wrong; our agent
reads the vendor's own usage statement and accrues the real number, and the flat rent it estimates from
code for free."

## Beat: document intake (upload any PDF)
Say: "A finance team shaped like **Kiteworks** or **eightfold.ai** gets bills as PDFs in email, not as
API calls. Drop one in: our own model reads it, the pipeline resolves the vendor, sizes it against
history, checks policy, and routes it to the right approver on the dashboard and in Slack."

## The close of the story
Say: "Those are three shapes of pain taken from the companies on Maximor's own wall. The rest of the
office of the CFO, revenue schedules, forecasting, the close checklist, the audit pack, is on the other
tabs, and it all runs on one ledger where nothing posts on a model's word alone."
